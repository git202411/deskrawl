The Steam Market is useful for filling a build gap, but it is also where rushed players overpay. Treat every listing as a data point, not a recommendation.

## How to check a listing

1. Search the exact item or rein name.
2. Compare several listings rather than the first result.
3. Read the full tooltip and roll values.
4. Check whether the item is needed by a current class build before selling it.

## What usually holds value

Rare reins, ancient legendaries, and items tied to a newly discovered boss route tend to attract the most demand. Prices can fall quickly after a patch, so do not buy a speculative item with currency needed for your next guaranteed upgrade.

## Market and progression

Use the market to bridge a specific gap, not to skip learning the game. The [items database](/items/) and [class builds](/builds/) show what a purchase actually enables.

<Accordion title="Can I sell a minion and keep its rein?">
Check the item tooltip before listing it. In Deskrawl, the minion and its rein can be separate parts of the value, so confirm exactly what the listing contains before you click sell.
