Warrior is the forgiving Deskrawl class: mistakes cost less, melee uptime is high, and a good defensive setup keeps an offline character alive through long sessions.

## Recommended order

Take the core melee skill, then a defensive cooldown. Upgrade health and armour before chasing a small damage roll. Once you can survive a full boss rotation, add attack speed and rage generation.

## Combat loop

Open with your defensive cooldown, stay behind or beside the boss, and spend rage during a damage window rather than on cooldown. Step out for area attacks instead of face-tanking every mechanic; the build is durable, not invulnerable.

## When to switch builds

If a boss has a long ranged phase, a pure melee setup can lose too much uptime. Keep one ranged-friendly weapon in storage and compare clear times after every major patch. Our [Deskrawl guide index](/guides/) tracks progression changes.
