Minions add a second layer to Deskrawl progression. A good capture can hold enemies, group a pack, or cover the damage window while your class moves out of danger.

## How capture works

Lower a target’s health, use the capture interaction when it becomes available, and check the resulting minion before leaving the area. Capture quality and the attached rein are separate: a common minion can still carry a useful rein.

## Roles to keep

- **Control:** slows or groups enemies for Hunter and Sorcerer.
- **Front line:** taunts or absorbs hits for Monk and Warrior.
- **Support:** improves recovery during long offline sessions.
- **Damage:** useful for early levelling, but often less valuable than utility in boss fights.

## Reins and the market

Reins are the part players most often misprice. Compare the exact name, rarity, and roll rather than searching only for the minion species. Our [Steam Market guide](/market/) explains the safe way to compare listings.

## Do not dismantle too early

Keep one copy of every new rein until you know which class you want to main. Storage is cheaper than rebuilding a rare capture after a patch changes a skill multiplier.
