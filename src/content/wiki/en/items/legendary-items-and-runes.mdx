Deskrawl item names are easy to collect and easy to misunderstand. The useful question is not “is this legendary?” but “does this effect improve the way my class actually clears content?”

## Legendary versus ancient

Legendary items provide a build-defining effect. Ancient versions usually add a stronger roll or an extra condition. Keep an ancient item if it fits your class; otherwise compare it with the current listings in the [Steam Market guide](/market/).

## Rune sets

Complete a useful two-piece effect before chasing a perfect four-piece set. A partial set that improves uptime is better than a full set whose bonus never triggers during your normal rotation.

## Blacksmith upgrades

Upgrade your current weapon first, then armour that fixes a survival breakpoint. Avoid draining rare materials into a temporary item one level below your next boss unlock. The [beginner guide](/guides/beginner-guide/) explains a safer early route.
